# Opportunity Assessment

## Purpose
Evaluate Opportunities for their **leverage on an Outcome**, the **strength of evidence** that
they exist, and the **uncertainty/consequence** around them — producing a comparable view without
forcing one universal score.

## When to use
- Before selecting a Target Opportunity.
- When new Evidence changes an Opportunity's standing.

## When NOT to use
- To discover or map Opportunities.
- To make the final selection (that is `opportunity-selection`, owned by the Product Owner).

## Inputs
- A mapped Opportunity Space; relevant Evidence/Learning; the target Outcome.

## Definition of Ready
- Opportunities are mapped and linked to an Outcome.

## Methodology selection
- **Lean Product** value/leverage thinking; **RICE / WSJF** where contextually useful.
- **Evidence strength** from the Learning Steward's appraisal.
- Do **not** hard-code one scoring formula — select the lens and show the working.

## Process
1. For each Opportunity, estimate leverage on the Outcome.
2. Weigh evidence of existence (strong/weak/none).
3. Note the key uncertainties and their consequence.
4. Produce a comparable, transparent assessment (not a single hidden number).

## Structured output
An assessment note per Opportunity (leverage, evidence strength, key uncertainty, consequence),
feeding `opportunity-selection` and `risk-assessment`.

## Quality criteria
- Method and rationale are explicit; comparisons are traceable to Evidence.

## Definition of Done
- Opportunities comparable on a consistent, transparent basis.

## Failure modes
- False precision from an arbitrary score.
- Confusing "interesting" with "high leverage on the Outcome".

## Escalation / governance
- Assessment informs but does not make the selection; the Product Owner selects the Target.

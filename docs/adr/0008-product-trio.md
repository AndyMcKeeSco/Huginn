# ADR 0008 — The Product Trio

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Continuous Discovery (Teresa Torres) centres a **Product Trio** — product, design and engineering
perspectives deciding together — so desirability, usability and feasibility are weighed jointly
rather than sequentially handed off.

## Decision
Huginn's core team is a **Product Trio: Product Owner, Designer, Engineer**, working **one shared
sprint** toward the intended Outcomes. The Product Owner proposes the Sprint Goal; Designer and
Engineer participate where their capabilities are required.

## Consequences
- One shared sprint (see `docs/operating-model/sprint-model.md`), not parallel per-discipline
  sprints.
- In v1 the Designer and Engineer are deliberately lightweight (ADR 0011); the Trio structure is
  in place so they can deepen without re-architecture.
- Specialist and governance agents support the Trio; they are not part of it.
